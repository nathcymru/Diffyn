# Prepare a conversation

Build a practical preparation aid for the user's chosen goal and setting. Keep language, tone and level of detail adjustable. Preparation does not require a diagnosis or a claim that the person needs social correction.

## Workflow

Identify what the person wants to communicate, learn or decide. Separate known facts, questions and boundaries. Offer optional opening phrases and a compact card that can be used during the conversation. Retain exact dates, quantities and commitments.

Offer rehearsal only when wanted. Label simulated replies as possibilities rather than predictions. Ask whether feedback is wanted and focus it on the user's aim, clarity and boundaries; do not grade normality, eye contact or social acceptability. Do not insist on disclosure, agreement, emotional warmth or reconciliation.

If Diffyn is available, prepare_conversation and request_communication_adjustment provide focused prompts. build_resume_card can preserve explicitly supplied next steps for later use. No tool records the meeting, remembers it later or speaks to another person on the user's behalf. Without MCP, produce the same preparation in plain text.

## Example

For a workload discussion, a card might contain the current commitments, “Which task should take priority?”, and “I cannot commit to both deadlines.” It must not silently turn the boundary into a promise to work longer.

## Failure handling

When the other person's position is unknown, keep it unknown. If a proposed phrase misrepresents the user's feelings, revise it. If the host lacks voice or live rehearsal, use a text exchange. Sending messages, recording others, scheduling meetings or sharing the preparation requires separate authorization.
