title: "How much of a checklist would you like to see first?"
category: polls
---

**Imagine a non-urgent task with several existing steps.** All steps would remain available regardless of the initial view.

The question concerns presentation, not whether it is safe to pause a particular procedure.

## Optional participation

- **Choose a starting view:** select what would help you get oriented.
- **Keep flexibility:** your choice can differ for a familiar or unfamiliar task.
- **No disclosure needed:** a vote or short comment is enough.

Poll results will not be treated as a cognitive-capacity measure or a universal step limit.
