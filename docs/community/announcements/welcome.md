**Welcome.** This is a space to improve communication tools without assuming that everyone needs the same format.

Diffyn provides local text tools, reusable prompts and reference guidance for MCP clients.
Your expressed preferences take priority over neurotype labels.

## Choose a starting point

- **Try something:** use a small, synthetic request from the [examples](https://github.com/nathcymru/Diffyn/blob/main/docs/examples.md).
- **Ask a question:** open a discussion in **Q&A**.
- **Suggest a change:** describe the problem and desired result in **Ideas**.
- **Share an example:** explain what it demonstrates in **Show and tell**.

## Participate in your own way

Reading without replying is welcome.
A short comment is enough, and you do not need to introduce yourself or disclose a diagnosis.

Please follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/nathcymru/Diffyn/blob/main/.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and keep other people's information private.
