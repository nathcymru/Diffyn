category: ideas

**Proposal for discussion:** improve how a host shows a short active group while keeping the full sequence easy to find.

The local checklist retains every source item in `all_steps`. Its `deferred_steps` contains everything outside the active window, including earlier, completed or skipped items as well as later requirements.
A display limit changes presentation; it does not prove that a procedure can safely pause there.

## Possible presentation choices

- **Clear counts:** distinguish unfinished later steps from completed or skipped items outside the active window.
- **An optional overview:** let the person inspect the full sequence without losing their place.
- **Persistent prerequisites:** keep important conditions visible across groups.
- **A clear distinction:** separate completed source checkboxes from an assumed completed task.

This is a host-presentation idea rather than a proposed change to the source text.
