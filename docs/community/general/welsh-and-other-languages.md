title: "Welsh and other languages: what makes an example useful?"
category: general
---

**Language support needs concrete examples.** A format that works in English may behave differently with another language or writing system.

Diffyn preserves submitted text, but its heuristics have limits.
The checklist recognizes some English and Welsh sequence markers; the clarity tool checks a finite set of English phrases.

## A helpful example includes

- **The language:** name it if that helps explain the issue.
- **A short passage:** use synthetic text that demonstrates one problem.
- **The expected behavior:** say whether the task concerns layout, ordering or interpretation.
- **Meaning to preserve:** identify any word, condition or punctuation that matters.

## Optional discussion
