title: "Share a reading or navigation barrier, at your level of detail"
category: general
---

**The host interface affects accessibility.** Diffyn supplies text; the client controls fonts, spacing, semantic rendering and assistive-technology behavior.

A server test cannot establish that a page works well with a screen reader, keyboard, zoom or a particular display.

## A small observation can help

- **Location:** identify the page, section or client feature.
- **Task:** describe what you wanted to find or do.
- **Barrier:** explain where navigation or understanding became harder.
- **Alternative:** suggest a different structure if one comes to mind.

You can name a device or assistive tool when it is relevant, but personal or diagnostic details are unnecessary.

## Optional discussion

Is there a structure that makes a long answer easier for you to navigate?
A short preference or a fictional example is enough.
