---
title: "Describe a communication preference without a diagnosis"
category: general
---

**A preference can be a practical request.** You do not need to explain why you want a particular format.

Examples include:

- **Order:** “Give me the answer first, then the explanation.”
- **Detail:** “Keep the technical terms and explain unfamiliar ones.”
- **Presentation:** “Use plain text without bold.”
- **Task support:** “Show the next action and keep the full sequence available.”

Some requests guide the host model, while the local tools expose a smaller set of [explicit settings](https://github.com/nathcymru/Diffyn/blob/main/docs/communication-contract.md#supported-preferences).
A preference can change with the task.

## Optional discussion

What is one phrase that clearly communicates your preferred format?
A made-up example is welcome, and no personal background is needed.
