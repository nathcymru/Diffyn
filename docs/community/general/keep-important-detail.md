---
title: "What information must survive a shorter-looking response?"
category: general
---

**Less visual density should not mean less useful information.** Diffyn's layout tool preserves wording rather than writing a summary.

Consider this synthetic instruction:

> Submit the form by 16:30 on Friday, but only after the reviewer approves it.

A shorter version could accidentally lose the time or the approval condition.
The clearer layout still needs both.

## Details worth checking

- **Exact values:** numbers, units and dates.
- **Conditions:** words such as “only after”, “unless” and “if”.
- **Negation:** instructions beginning with “do not”.
- **Uncertainty:** qualifications that change how confidently someone should act.
