# Charity artwork

**Ownership:** charity names and logos belong to their respective organisations
and are excluded from Diffyn's MIT license.

**Purpose:** the artwork identifies charities on the
[donation page](../../../.github/DONATE.md) and does not imply endorsement of Diffyn.

## Original assets

- **National Autistic Society:** [logo](https://www.autism.org.uk/Content/assets/general/img/logo.svg) and [icon](https://www.autism.org.uk/content/assets/favicon/favicon-32x32.png).
- **British Dyslexia Association:** [logo](https://cdn.bdadyslexia.org.uk/assets/v20261022_084056/img/logo.svg) and [icon](https://cdn.bdadyslexia.org.uk/assets/v20261022_084056/img/apple-touch-icon.png).
- **ADHD UK:** [logo](https://adhduk.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/ADHD-UK-Logo-Clear-Background-480x136.png) and [icon](https://adhduk.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/cropped-ADHD-UK-Square-32x32.jpg).

## Presentation

- **Logo panels** retain the original artwork on a white background for light and dark themes.
- **Donation buttons** use [Shields.io](https://shields.io/) with the charities' original icons.
