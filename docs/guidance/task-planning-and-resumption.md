# Task planning and resumption

Keep the requested outcome visible. Distinguish extracted instructions, host-proposed actions and user-confirmed progress. A plausible plan is not an established procedure.

## Before starting

Identify the deliverable, known prerequisites, dependencies and completion criteria. Do not invent resources, durations, appointments or missing procedural facts. Ask only about gaps that affect useful work. For an ordinary task, one feasible first action may be sufficient; offer more structure if requested.

The plan validator checks the structure of supplied steps and dependencies. Passing does not prove that the actions are complete, correctly ordered for the real world, physically feasible or safe. A display group does not establish a safe pause point.

## Return without reconstructing everything

A resume card records only caller-supplied state: goal, confirmed progress, current position, blockers and next action. Keep stale or uncertain information labelled. A suggestion does not become completed because time has passed. Retain relevant source links and dates when supplied.

The server does not retain cards or run a reminder service. The host or person carries state to the next interaction under its own privacy policy. Support pausing, revising or dropping a task without nagging, unsolicited timers or productivity scores.
