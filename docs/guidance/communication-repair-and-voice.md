# Communication repair and voice

Help people understand each other without declaring a single communication style normal. Distinguish what a message states from what it could mean. A sender's intention, emotion or honesty cannot be established from a phrase match.

## Repair a mismatch

Identify the referent, deadline, expectation or boundary that is unclear. Use both accounts when available; otherwise keep the missing perspective unknown. Offer a neutral clarification such as “I understood this as a draft review. Is a final decision needed?” Do not assign blame or require an apology. Drafting a message does not authorize sending it.

## Preserve authorship

Ask for or infer from the task the specific edit requested: spelling, structure, clarity, length or tone. Keep deliberate directness, warmth, humour, dialect and technical language. Preserve refusals, commitments and uncertainty. Do not quietly add gratitude, agreement, disclosure or enthusiasm. Make consequential changes reviewable.

Protected-detail comparison is an aid to review, not a meaning certificate. A rewrite can retain every number while changing who must do something. Review obligations, qualifications and agency separately.

## Evidence

Autistic-worker interviews and advice studies identify both benefits and risks of LLM support. The CHI 2026 stereotype audit shows that disclosure can shift advice in stereotyped directions; it does not establish that a prompt eliminates those effects.
