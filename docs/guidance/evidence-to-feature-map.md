# Evidence to feature map

Evidence motivates design choices; it does not establish that Diffyn improves comprehension, executive function or health. Full metadata and sample limits are in the research report and bibliography.

| Evidence | Design response | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W3C COGA, 2021: user control, clear steps, alternatives and reduced memory demands | Preference choice, layered explanations, explicit briefs and resume cards | Supplemental guidance; not a clinical trial or WCAG certification |
| Jang et al., CHI 2024: 11 autistic participants and a practitioner identified differing experiences of LLM advice | Clarification, source-based repair and review of advice | Small exploratory study; preference is not accuracy |
| Çarik et al., January 2025: discussions across 61 Reddit communities | Discoverable prompts and workflows that accept imperfect input | Self-reported online experiences; not population prevalence |
| Jamshed et al., ASSETS 2025: 19 neurodivergent students | Voice preservation, flexible pacing and task re-entry | One institution; interview accounts rather than an intervention trial |
| Pinto et al., November 2025: 13 adults in a seven-day diary study | Task initiation and manageable next actions | Exploratory self-report; no clinical efficacy claim |
| Wohn et al., CHI 2026: 345,000 outputs from six models and 11 autistic interviewees | Explicit preferences instead of diagnostic presets; preserve agency and ambitions | Tested models and scenarios; no proof that these instructions remove bias |
| Kan et al., May 2026: participatory discussions with 20 autistic adults in Singapore | Reciprocal communication and concrete adjustment requests | Context-specific qualitative findings; not chatbot outcome evidence |

Protected-detail comparison, plan validation and response-contract checking are engineering controls. They need software tests and failure review. Meaning, usefulness, language coverage and user acceptance require separate evaluation with people who choose to participate.
